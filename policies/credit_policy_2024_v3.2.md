# Credit Policy 2024 v3.2

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024
**Category:** Financial
**Last Updated:** March 1, 2024

## Section 1: Overview

This policy governs the issuance of credits and compensation to customers by customer service agents. All credits must comply with the authorization levels and approval workflows defined herein.

## Section 2: Credit Types

Credits may be issued for the following reasons:
- Service disruptions or outages
- Billing errors
- Customer retention
- Goodwill gestures
- Promotional adjustments

## Section 3: Authorization Levels

### 3.1 Agent Self-Service Credits
Agents may issue credits up to $50 without additional approval, regardless of account tenure.

### 3.2 Tenure-Based Authorization
For credits exceeding $50, the following tenure requirements apply:
- **Accounts with 90+ days tenure:** Agents may issue credits up to $500
- **Accounts with less than 90 days tenure:** Credits over $50 require supervisor approval

### 3.3 Supervisor Approval Required
The following always require supervisor approval:
- Single credits exceeding $500
- Credits for accounts flagged for fraud review
- Credits that would exceed $1,000 total in a 30-day period

## Section 4: Credit Rules

### Section 4.1 — Credit Authorization Threshold (CREDIT-001)
**Severity:** High
**Rule:** Credits over $50 require 90+ days account tenure. Agents must not offer credits exceeding $50 to accounts with less than 90 days of tenure without supervisor approval.
**Keywords:** credit, compensation, offer you
**Corrective Action:** Reduce credit to $50 maximum and offer to escalate for additional review.

### Section 4.3 — Maximum Credit Amount (CREDIT-002)
**Severity:** Critical
**Rule:** Single credit cannot exceed $500 without supervisor approval. Any credit request above $500 must be escalated immediately.
**Keywords:** credit, compensation, offer you
**Corrective Action:** Escalate to supervisor immediately. Do not issue the credit.

## Section 5: Documentation

All credits issued must be documented with:
- Reason for credit
- Customer account number
- Agent ID
- Supervisor approval (if required)
- Timestamp

## Section 6: Compliance

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Corrective coaching for first offense
- Written warning for repeat offenses
- Termination for willful circumvention of approval workflows
