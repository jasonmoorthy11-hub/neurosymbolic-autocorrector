# Escalation Policy 2024 v1.0

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024
**Category:** Escalation
**Last Updated:** January 15, 2024

## Section 1: Mandatory Escalations

### Section 1.1 — Legal Threat Escalation (ESC-001)
**Severity:** Critical
**Rule:** Legal threats or regulatory concerns require immediate supervisor review. Agents must not respond to the substance of a legal or regulatory matter.
**Keywords:** legal action, attorney, lawyer, lawsuit, litigation, regulatory, regulator, sue
**Corrective Action:** Stop the reply and route the conversation to a supervisor.

### Section 1.2 — What Counts as a Legal Threat
A conversation is treated as a legal threat when the customer or the agent mentions:
- Taking or considering legal action
- An attorney, lawyer or law firm
- A complaint to a regulator or a consumer-protection agency

## Section 2: Handoff

When escalating, the agent records:
- The customer's stated concern, in the customer's words
- The account number and the conversation ID
- The time of the escalation
