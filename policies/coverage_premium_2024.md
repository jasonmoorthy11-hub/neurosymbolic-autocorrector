# Premium Plan 2024 — Coverage Details

**Product:** Premium 2024
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024

## Coverage Summary

| Coverage Category | Annual Limit | Source Page |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency Dental | $5,000 | Page 12 |
| Emergency Room | $25,000 | Page 8 |

## Emergency Dental Coverage
**Annual Limit:** $5,000
**Source:** Policy_Document_Premium_2024.pdf, Page 12

Enhanced emergency dental coverage including:
- All Standard plan emergency dental benefits
- Emergency oral surgery
- Emergency periodontal treatment
- Temporary and permanent restorations
- Second opinion consultations

## Emergency Room Coverage
**Annual Limit:** $25,000
**Source:** Policy_Document_Premium_2024.pdf, Page 8

Enhanced emergency room coverage including:
- All Standard plan ER benefits
- Specialist consultations
- Advanced imaging (MRI, PET scan)
- Extended observation up to 48 hours
- Emergency surgery
- Air ambulance transport (up to $5,000)
