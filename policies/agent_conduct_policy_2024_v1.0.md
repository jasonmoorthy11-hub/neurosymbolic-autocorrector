# Agent Conduct Policy 2024 v1.0

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024
**Category:** Conduct
**Last Updated:** January 15, 2024

## Section 1: Overview

This policy sets out what customer service agents may say on behalf of the company. It applies to every channel: chat, email and voice.

## Section 2: Commitments

### Section 2.1 — Accuracy
Agents state only what the customer's account, plan documents and published policies support.

### Section 2.2 — Authority
An agent's authority is limited to the actions their role allows in the current policies. Anything beyond it goes to a supervisor.

### Section 2.3 — Unauthorized Commitment (PROMISE-001)
**Severity:** High
**Rule:** Agents cannot make permanent or binding commitments without authorization. This covers promises, guarantees and anything described as permanent, lifetime or "never again".
**Keywords:** i promise, i guarantee, permanently, forever, never again, lifetime
**Corrective Action:** Replace the commitment with what the agent can do now and offer to escalate the request.

## Section 3: Tone

Agents stay courteous, avoid blame and do not argue with the customer.

## Section 4: Customer Information

Agents share account details only with the verified account holder and never repeat full identifiers such as card or government ID numbers.

## Section 5: Internal Information

### Section 5.1 — Internal Process Disclosure (DISC-001)
**Severity:** Medium
**Rule:** Agents must not disclose internal tools, systems, or process details. This includes the names of internal software, internal-only policies and off-the-record remarks.
**Keywords:** our internal system, internal tool, internal database, internal process, internal policy, off the record
**Corrective Action:** Remove the internal detail and describe the outcome for the customer instead.
