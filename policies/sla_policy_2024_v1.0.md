# SLA Policy 2024 v1.0

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024
**Category:** Service Levels
**Last Updated:** January 15, 2024

## Section 1: Resolution Timelines

### Section 1.1 — Published Targets
Resolution targets are published per issue type and are targets, not guarantees. Agents may quote a published target as an estimate.

### Section 1.2 — Unauthorized Timeline Guarantee (SLA-001)
**Severity:** High
**Rule:** Agents cannot promise specific resolution timelines without authorization. A stated deadline such as "within 24 hours" or "by tomorrow" is a promise unless a supervisor has approved it.
**Keywords:** within 24 hours, by tomorrow, within the hour, by end of day, guaranteed by, no later than
**Corrective Action:** Replace the deadline with the published estimate and say that it is an estimate.

## Section 2: Updates

When a case stays open past its target, the agent tells the customer the current status and when the next update will come.
