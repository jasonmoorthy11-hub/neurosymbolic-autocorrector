# Standard Plan 2024 — Coverage Details

**Product:** Standard 2024
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024

## Coverage Summary

| Coverage Category | Annual Limit (copay: per visit) | Source Page |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency Dental | $2,500 | Page 12 |
| Emergency Room | $10,000 | Page 8 |
| Prescription Drugs | $5,000 | Page 15 |
| Specialist Visit Copay | $40 | Page 22 |

## Emergency Dental Coverage
**Annual Limit:** $2,500
**Source:** Policy_Document_Standard_2024.pdf, Page 12

Covers emergency dental procedures including:
- Emergency extractions
- Treatment of dental trauma
- Emergency root canal therapy
- Temporary restorations

**Exclusions:**
- Routine dental cleanings
- Cosmetic procedures
- Orthodontic treatment

## Emergency Room Coverage
**Annual Limit:** $10,000
**Source:** Policy_Document_Standard_2024.pdf, Page 8

Covers emergency room visits at in-network facilities including:
- Physician evaluation
- Diagnostic tests (X-ray, blood work, CT scan)
- Emergency procedures
- Observation stays up to 23 hours

## Prescription Drugs Coverage
**Annual Limit:** $5,000
**Source:** Policy_Document_Standard_2024.pdf, Page 15

Covers prescription medications on the approved formulary:
- Tier 1 (Generic): $10 copay
- Tier 2 (Preferred Brand): $30 copay
- Tier 3 (Non-Preferred): $50 copay
- Specialty medications: 20% coinsurance up to annual limit

## Specialist Visits
**Copay:** $40 per visit
**Source:** Policy_Document_Standard_2024.pdf, Page 22

Applies to in-network specialist office visits with a referral:
- The member pays the $40 copay at the time of the visit
- Out-of-network specialist visits are not covered by the copay
