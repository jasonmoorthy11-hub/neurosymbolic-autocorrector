# Refund Policy 2024 v2.1

**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024
**Category:** Financial
**Last Updated:** March 15, 2024

## Section 1: Overview

This policy governs the processing of customer refunds. All refund requests must be evaluated against the criteria defined in this document.

## Section 2: Refund Rules

### Section 2.1 — Refund Window (REFUND-001)
**Severity:** High
**Rule:** Refunds must be processed within 30 days of purchase. Agents must verify the purchase date before initiating any refund.
**Keywords:** refund, money back, accept the return, process the return
**Corrective Action:** Verify purchase date and inform customer of refund eligibility based on the 30-day window.

### Section 2.2 — Refund Approval Authority
- **Up to $100:** Agent may process independently
- **$100-$500:** Team lead approval required
- **Over $500:** Manager approval required

## Section 3: Policy Exceptions

### Section 3.1 — Policy Exception Authorization (REFUND-002)
**Severity:** Critical
**Rule:** Policy exceptions require supervisor approval. Agents must not grant, promise or imply an exception to this policy on their own authority.
**Keywords:** make an exception, policy exception, override the policy, outside the window
**Corrective Action:** Do not grant the exception. Escalate to a supervisor for review.

### Section 3.2 — Situations Treated as Exceptions
The following always count as policy exceptions, regardless of amount:
- Refunds requested outside the 30-day window
- Refunds for digital products after download
- Refunds for customized items
- Refunds for items not in original condition

## Section 4: Refund Methods

Refunds should be processed to the original payment method:
- Credit card: 3-5 business days
- Debit card: 5-7 business days
- Bank transfer: 7-10 business days
- Store credit: Immediate

## Section 5: Documentation

All refunds must be documented with:
- Original order number
- Reason for refund
- Refund amount
- Processing method
- Agent ID and timestamp
